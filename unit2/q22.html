Q22. Describe the Concept of Cyberstalking, the Methods Used by Stalkers, and the Psychological Impact on Victims.

Introduction

Cyberstalking means repeatedly monitoring, contacting, threatening or harassing a person through the internet or digital technology.

It may occur through social media, emails, messaging apps and other online platforms.

1. Methods Used by Cyberstalkers

a) Repeated Messages and Threats

Sending unwanted messages, abusive comments or threats to frighten or disturb the victim.

b) Monitoring Social Media

Repeatedly checking the victim's posts, photographs, friends and online activities.

c) Creating Fake Accounts

Using fake profiles to hide identity or contact the victim after being blocked.

d) Spreading False Information

Posting false rumours or embarrassing information to damage the victim's reputation.

e) Misuse of Personal Information

Collecting or sharing the victim's personal details or photographs without permission.

f) Impersonation

Creating accounts in the victim's name and pretending to be that person online.

2. Psychological Impact on Victims

a) Fear and Anxiety

Victims may constantly worry about the stalker's next actions.

b) Mental Stress

Repeated harassment and threats may cause continuous stress and emotional distress.

c) Loss of Sense of Safety

Victims may feel unsafe even at home because online harassment can occur at any time.

d) Loss of Confidence

Repeated harassment may reduce self-esteem and personal confidence.

e) Social Isolation

Victims may avoid social media, friends or online communication because of fear.

f) Sleep and Concentration Problems

Constant worry may disturb sleep and affect concentration on studies or work.

Conclusion

Cyberstalking involves repeated online monitoring, unwanted contact and harassment. Stalkers may use fake accounts, threats, impersonation and misuse of personal information. Victims may experience fear, anxiety, stress, loss of confidence and social isolation.
