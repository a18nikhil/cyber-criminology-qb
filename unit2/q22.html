Q22. Describe the Concept of Cyberstalking, the Methods Used by Stalkers, and the Psychological Impact on Victims.

Introduction

Cyberstalking means repeatedly following, monitoring, contacting, threatening or harassing a person through the internet or digital technology.

It may take place through social media, emails, messaging apps, online forums and other digital platforms. The victim may feel that the stalker is constantly watching or following their online activities.

1. Methods Used by Cyberstalkers

Cyberstalkers may use different methods to disturb or monitor victims.

a) Repeated Messages

The stalker may repeatedly send unwanted messages, emails or comments even after the victim asks them to stop.

b) Monitoring Social Media

The stalker may constantly check the victim's posts, photographs, friends and online activities.

c) Creating Fake Accounts

Fake profiles may be created to contact the victim after being blocked or to hide the stalker's real identity.

d) Threats and Harassment

The stalker may send threatening, abusive or frightening messages to the victim.

e) Spreading False Information

False stories, rumours or embarrassing information may be posted online to damage the victim's reputation.

f) Misuse of Personal Information

The stalker may collect or misuse personal information, photographs or other details about the victim.

g) Impersonation

The stalker may pretend to be the victim by creating fake accounts in the victim's name.

2. Common Pattern of Cyberstalking

Finding the Victim Online
↓
Monitoring Online Activities
↓
Repeated Unwanted Contact
↓
Harassment or Threats
↓
Fear and Distress to the Victim

3. Psychological Impact on Victims

Cyberstalking can seriously affect the mental and emotional well-being of victims.

a) Fear and Anxiety

Victims may constantly worry about what the stalker will do next.

b) Stress

Repeated unwanted contact and threats can create continuous mental stress.

c) Loss of Sense of Safety

Victims may feel unsafe even at home because cyberstalking can occur at any time through digital devices.

d) Loss of Confidence

Repeated harassment may reduce the victim's confidence and self-esteem.

e) Social Isolation

Victims may stop using social media or avoid communicating with other people because of fear.

f) Sleep and Concentration Problems

Constant worry may disturb sleep and make it difficult to concentrate on studies or work.

Conclusion

Cyberstalking is a serious form of online victimization involving repeated monitoring, unwanted contact, harassment or threats through digital platforms. Stalkers may use fake accounts, repeated messages, threats, impersonation and misuse of personal information. It can cause serious psychological effects such as fear, anxiety, stress, loss of confidence and social isolation.
