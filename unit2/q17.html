Q17. List and Explain Any Three Criminological Predictors of Digital Piracy.

Introduction

Digital piracy means illegally downloading, copying or sharing copyrighted content such as movies, music, software, games and e-books.

Criminological predictors are factors that help explain why a person may be more likely to take part in digital piracy.

Any three important predictors are:

1. Peer Influence

Peer influence means the influence of friends or other people in a person's group.

A person may be more likely to commit digital piracy when:

  • Friends also download pirated content.
  • Friends share pirated movies, music or software.
  • Piracy is considered normal within the group.
  • Friends encourage the person to use pirated content.

For example, if most students in a friend group regularly share pirated movies, another student may also start doing it.

2. Low Self-Control

Low self-control means difficulty in controlling one's actions and avoiding risky or illegal behaviour.

A person with low self-control may choose piracy because:

  • Pirated content is easily available.
  • It provides quick access to movies, games or music.
  • The person may not think about future consequences.
  • The person may prefer getting something free instead of paying for it.

Thus, the desire for quick and easy access can increase the chance of digital piracy.

3. Neutralization

Neutralization means giving excuses to make illegal behaviour seem acceptable.

People involved in digital piracy may tell themselves:

  • "Everyone downloads pirated content."
  • "No one is really getting hurt."
  • "The original product is too expensive."
  • "Big companies already earn enough money."

Such excuses can reduce feelings of guilt and make it easier for a person to continue piracy.

Conclusion

Three important criminological predictors of digital piracy are peer influence, low self-control and neutralization. Friends can make piracy appear normal, low self-control can encourage quick illegal choices, and neutralization allows offenders to justify their actions. These factors help explain why some internet users take part in digital piracy.
