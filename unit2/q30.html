Q30. Describe P2P Network Users, Their Role in Digital Piracy, and the Associated Risks and Legal Consequences.

Introduction

P2P (Peer-to-Peer) is a network that allows users to share digital files directly with each other through the internet.

P2P network users can both download and upload files, acting as receivers and providers without depending on a central server for every transfer.

P2P technology itself is legal, but sharing copyrighted content without permission may be illegal.

1. Role of P2P Users in Digital Piracy

a) Illegal Downloading

Users may download copyrighted movies, music, software or games without permission.

b) Uploading and Sharing

Users may upload copyrighted files and make them available to others.

c) Wide Distribution

A single copyrighted file can be shared among many users through a P2P network.

d) Fast Sharing

Copyrighted content can spread quickly because multiple users can share the same file.

2. Risks Associated with P2P Networks

a) Malware

Downloaded files may contain viruses, spyware or other harmful software.

b) Data Theft

Malicious files may steal passwords, banking details or personal information.

c) Fake Files

Downloaded files may contain harmful or different content instead of the expected file.

d) Privacy Risks

P2P software may expose users' IP addresses or accidentally share personal files.

3. Legal Consequences

a) Legal Notices

Users may receive copyright complaints or warning notices.

b) Fines or Compensation

Users may have to pay fines or compensation, depending on applicable laws.

c) Civil Lawsuits

Copyright owners may take legal action against users for copyright infringement.

d) Criminal Penalties

Certain serious copyright violations may result in criminal punishment under applicable laws.

Conclusion

P2P networks allow users to share files directly with one another. However, unauthorized sharing of copyrighted content may lead to digital piracy. Users may also face malware, data theft, privacy risks and legal consequences.
