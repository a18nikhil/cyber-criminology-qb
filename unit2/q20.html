Q20. Explain the Criminological Predictors of Digital Piracy Among Internet Users.

Introduction

Digital piracy means illegally downloading, copying or sharing copyrighted content such as movies, music, software and games.

Criminological predictors are factors that help explain why some internet users are more likely to participate in digital piracy.

1. Peer Influence

Peer influence means the influence of friends or other people in a group.

When friends regularly download or share pirated content, a person may copy their behaviour. This can make piracy seem normal and acceptable.

2. Low Self-Control

Low self-control means difficulty controlling one's actions and thinking about future consequences.

People with low self-control may choose pirated content because it is free and easily available. They may focus on immediate benefits and ignore legal consequences.

3. Neutralization

Neutralization means giving excuses to justify illegal behaviour.

Internet users may justify piracy by saying:

  • "Everyone does it."
  • "No one is getting hurt."
  • "Movies and software are too expensive."
  • "Companies already make enough money."

Such excuses can reduce feelings of guilt and encourage piracy.

4. Opportunity and Easy Internet Access

Easy access to the internet creates more opportunities for digital piracy.

Illegal websites, file-sharing platforms and downloading tools make it easier for users to find and share pirated content.

5. Low Fear of Punishment

Some users believe they are unlikely to be caught or punished for digital piracy.

They may think their activities cannot be traced or that authorities will not take action against them. This belief can encourage illegal downloading and sharing.

6. Attitude Towards Piracy

A person's attitude towards piracy can influence their behaviour.

Users who believe piracy is harmless or not a serious offence may be more willing to download or share copyrighted content without permission.

Conclusion

Digital piracy can be influenced by peer influence, low self-control, neutralization, easy opportunities, low fear of punishment and favourable attitudes towards piracy. These factors help explain why some internet users participate in digital piracy.
