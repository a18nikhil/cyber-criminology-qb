Q7. Examine Adolescent Online Victimization, Including Its Causes, Patterns and Consequences.

Introduction

Adolescent online victimization means harm experienced by teenagers through the internet or digital platforms.

It includes cyberbullying, harassment, stalking, online scams and exploitation.

1. Causes of Adolescent Online Victimization

a) Lack of Awareness

Teenagers may not fully understand online dangers and may easily trust strangers.

b) Sharing Personal Information

Publicly sharing photographs, phone numbers or locations can expose teenagers to cybercriminals.

c) Interaction with Strangers

Unknown people may use fake identities to gain trust and misuse personal information.

d) High Social Media Use

Frequent social media activity may increase exposure to harmful users and online harassment.

e) Weak Privacy and Security

Public accounts, weak passwords and poor privacy settings can increase vulnerability.

2. Common Patterns of Online Victimization

Adolescents may experience different forms of online victimization:

  • Cyberbullying: Repeatedly insulting or threatening someone online.
  • Online Harassment: Sending abusive or unwanted messages.
  • Cyberstalking: Repeatedly monitoring or contacting someone online.
  • Online Grooming: Building trust with a minor for sexual exploitation.
  • Online Scams: Tricking users into giving money or information.
  • Image Misuse: Sharing private photographs without permission.

3. Consequences of Adolescent Online Victimization

a) Emotional Effects

Victims may experience fear, anxiety, sadness or stress.

b) Social Effects

Teenagers may avoid friends, family and social activities.

c) Educational Effects

Victimization may cause poor concentration and lower academic performance.

d) Privacy Damage

Private photographs, messages or personal information may be exposed or misused.

e) Safety Concerns

Online threats, stalking or exploitation may create risks to personal safety.

Conclusion

Adolescent online victimization may result from lack of awareness, unsafe online interactions and weak privacy. It can cause emotional, social, educational and safety problems.
