Q4. Define Digital Piracy and Explain Its Common Forms.

Introduction

Digital piracy means illegally copying, downloading, sharing or distributing copyrighted digital content without the permission of its owner.

Digital content includes movies, music, software, games, e-books and other copyrighted material.

1. Common Forms of Digital Piracy

a) Software Piracy

Software piracy means illegally copying, installing or sharing paid software without a valid licence.

Example: Installing one paid software copy on many computers without permission.

b) Music Piracy

Music piracy means illegally downloading, copying or sharing copyrighted music without permission.

Example: Downloading copyrighted songs from an unauthorized website.

c) Movie and Video Piracy

Movie and video piracy means illegally downloading, copying, streaming or sharing copyrighted movies and videos.

Example: Watching a newly released movie on an illegal streaming website.

d) Game Piracy

Game piracy means illegally downloading, copying or distributing copyrighted video games.

Example: Downloading a cracked version of a paid computer game for free.

e) E-book Piracy

E-book piracy means illegally copying or sharing copyrighted books and other digital publications.

Example: Uploading a paid e-book online without the owner's permission.

f) P2P File-Sharing Piracy

P2P (Peer-to-Peer) file sharing allows users to share files directly with each other. It becomes piracy when copyrighted files are shared without permission.

Example: Sharing copyrighted movies or music through a P2P network without permission.

Conclusion

Digital piracy involves the unauthorized use or distribution of copyrighted digital content. Its common forms include software piracy, music piracy, movie piracy, game piracy, e-book piracy and P2P file-sharing piracy.
