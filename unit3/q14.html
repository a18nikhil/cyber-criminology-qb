Q14. Discuss Cyber Vandalism as a Form of Cybercrime, Covering Its Motivations and Consequences.

Introduction

Cyber vandalism is a form of cybercrime in which a person deliberately damages, changes, destroys or disrupts digital systems, websites, data or online services without permission.

It is similar to physical vandalism, but the damage is done to digital property instead of physical property.

1. Common Examples of Cyber Vandalism

Cyber vandalism may include:

  • Changing the content of a website without permission.
  • Deleting or damaging digital files.
  • Disrupting websites or online services.
  • Replacing a website's original page with offensive messages or images.
  • Damaging computer systems or networks.
  • Intentionally spreading harmful programs to cause damage.

2. Motivations Behind Cyber Vandalism

People may commit cyber vandalism for different reasons.

a) Fun or Excitement

Some offenders attack systems simply for entertainment, curiosity or the excitement of causing disruption.

b) Revenge

A person may damage a website, files or computer system because of anger or a personal conflict.

Example: A former employee damages company data after leaving the organization.

c) Showing Technical Skills

Some offenders may attack websites or systems to prove their computer skills or gain recognition among others.

d) Political or Social Reasons

Attackers may damage or change websites to spread a political or social message.

e) Attention and Recognition

Some offenders want publicity or recognition from online groups and may commit cyber vandalism to attract attention.

f) Causing Disruption

The main aim may simply be to disturb the normal functioning of an organization, website or online service.

3. Consequences of Cyber Vandalism

a) Financial Loss

Organizations may have to spend money repairing damaged systems, restoring data and improving security.

b) Loss of Data

Important files or information may be changed, damaged or permanently deleted.

c) Disruption of Services

Websites, networks or online services may become unavailable or stop working properly.

d) Damage to Reputation

A damaged or altered website can reduce people's trust in an organization.

e) Privacy and Security Risks

Cyber vandalism may expose private or sensitive information and create further security problems.

f) Legal Consequences for Offenders

Cyber vandalism may lead to legal action, fines or other penalties depending on the nature of the offence and applicable law.

Conclusion

Cyber vandalism is the intentional damage, alteration or disruption of digital property. Offenders may be motivated by fun, revenge, recognition, political reasons or a desire to cause disruption. Its consequences can include financial loss, loss of data, interruption of services, security problems and damage to reputation.
