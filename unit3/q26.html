Q26. Discuss the Connection Between Cybercrime Victimization and Human Rights, and Highlight Which Rights Are Most Violated.

Introduction

Cybercrime victimization occurs when a person suffers harm because of crimes committed through computers, mobile devices or the internet.

Cybercrime is also connected with human rights because online crimes can affect a person's privacy, dignity, safety, freedom and property.

1. Connection Between Cybercrime and Human Rights

Human rights protect the basic freedom, dignity and security of every person.

When cybercriminals steal personal information, threaten people, spread private content or damage digital property, they may interfere with these rights.

For example:

↓

↓

2. Right to Privacy

The right to privacy is one of the most commonly affected rights in cyberspace.

It may be violated through:

  • Hacking personal accounts
  • Stealing personal information
  • Spying on online activities
  • Sharing private photos without permission
  • Unauthorized access to emails or messages

Example: Hacking someone's email account and reading private messages without permission.

3. Right to Dignity and Reputation

Every person has the right to be treated with dignity and to protect their reputation.

Cyberbullying, online harassment, fake posts and sharing embarrassing content can harm a person's dignity and reputation.

Example: Posting false and insulting information about someone on social media.

4. Right to Security and Safety

People have a right to personal security and protection from threats and abuse.

Cyberstalking, online threats and repeated harassment can make victims feel unsafe.

Example: A cyberstalker repeatedly sends threatening messages to a victim.

5. Right to Property

Cybercrime may affect a person's money or digital property.

This can happen through:

  • Online financial fraud
  • Identity theft
  • Theft of digital information
  • Unauthorized financial transactions
  • Damage to digital files

Example: A cybercriminal steals banking details and transfers money from the victim's account.

6. Freedom of Expression

People should be able to express themselves online without unlawful threats or harassment.

Cyberbullying, trolling, threats and online abuse may cause victims to stop expressing their opinions or participating online.

7. Right to Equality and Non-Discrimination

Online abuse may target people because of their gender, race, religion or other personal characteristics.

Such targeted harassment can affect the person's right to equal treatment and freedom from discrimination.

8. Major Rights Violated by Cybercrime

Human Right Example of Cybercrime
Right to Privacy Hacking and data theft
Right to Dignity and Reputation Cyberbullying and false posts
Right to Security Cyberstalking and online threats
Right to Property Online fraud and financial theft
Freedom of Expression Threats that silence victims
Right to Equality Discriminatory online harassment

Conclusion

Cybercrime victimization and human rights are closely connected because cybercrime can harm a person's privacy, dignity, security, property, freedom and equality. Among these, the right to privacy, dignity, security and property are commonly affected. Therefore, protecting people from cybercrime is also important for protecting their human rights.
