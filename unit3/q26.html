Q26. Discuss the Connection Between Cybercrime Victimization and Human Rights, and Highlight Which Rights Are Most Violated.

Introduction

Cybercrime victimization occurs when a person suffers harm through crimes committed using computers, mobile devices or the internet.

Cybercrime is connected with human rights because it can affect a person's privacy, dignity, security, property and freedom.

1. Connection Between Cybercrime Victimization and Human Rights

Human rights protect the basic freedom, dignity and security of every person.

Cybercrimes such as hacking, online fraud, cyberbullying and cyberstalking can interfere with these rights and cause financial, emotional or social harm.

2. Human Rights Most Affected by Cybercrime

a) Right to Privacy

Hacking accounts, stealing personal information or sharing private content without permission can violate a person's privacy.

b) Right to Dignity and Reputation

Cyberbullying, online harassment and spreading false information can damage a person's dignity and reputation.

c) Right to Security and Safety

Cyberstalking, online threats and repeated harassment can create fear and make victims feel unsafe.

d) Right to Property

Online fraud, identity theft and unauthorized financial transactions can cause financial or property loss.

e) Freedom of Expression

Online threats and harassment may discourage people from expressing their opinions or participating in online discussions.

f) Right to Equality and Non-Discrimination

Online harassment targeting people because of their gender, religion or race can undermine equality and fair treatment.

Conclusion

Cybercrime victimization can affect several human rights, especially privacy, dignity, security and property. Protecting people from cybercrime is therefore important for safeguarding their rights.
