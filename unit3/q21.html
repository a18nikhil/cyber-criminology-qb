Q21. Define Cyber Vandalism and Explain Its Common Forms.

Introduction

Cyber vandalism is the intentional damage, alteration, destruction or disruption of digital property without permission.

Digital property may include websites, computer systems, networks, software and data. It is similar to physical vandalism, but the damage takes place in the digital world.

1. Website Defacement

Website defacement means changing the appearance or content of a website without the owner's permission.

An offender may replace the original webpage with:

  • Offensive messages
  • Unwanted images
  • Political messages
  • False information

Example: An attacker changes the homepage of an organization's website and displays an unwanted message.

2. Data Destruction

It involves intentionally deleting, changing or damaging digital data.

Important documents, records or databases may be destroyed or made unusable.

Example: An attacker deletes important files from a company's computer system.

3. Website or Service Disruption

Cyber vandals may attempt to make a website or online service unavailable to its users.

This can disturb the normal activities of businesses, institutions or other organizations.

Example: An attack causes an organization's website to become unavailable for several hours.

4. Malware-Based Vandalism

Malicious software may be used to intentionally damage computers or data.

It may:

  • Delete files
  • Damage software
  • Change system settings
  • Stop computers from working properly

5. Unauthorized Modification of Digital Content

An offender may gain unauthorized access and change digital information.

Example: Changing information stored on a website or database without permission.

6. Social Media or Online Account Vandalism

An offender may gain unauthorized access to an online account and change or delete its content.

Example: A hacked social media account is used to post offensive content or delete the owner's posts.

7. Common Forms at a Glance

Form Meaning
Website Defacement Changing a website without permission
Data Destruction Deleting or damaging digital files
Service Disruption Making online services unavailable
Malware-Based Vandalism Using malware to cause digital damage
Unauthorized Modification Changing digital information without permission
Account Vandalism Damaging or changing an online account

Conclusion

Cyber vandalism is the deliberate damage or disruption of digital property. Its common forms include website defacement, data destruction, service disruption, malware-based damage, unauthorized modification and online account vandalism. Such acts can cause loss of data, financial damage and interruption of digital services.
