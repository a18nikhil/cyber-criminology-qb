Q21. Define Cyber Vandalism and Explain Its Common Forms.

Introduction

Cyber vandalism is the intentional damage, alteration, destruction or disruption of digital property without permission.

Digital property may include websites, computer systems, networks, software and data. It is similar to physical vandalism, but the damage takes place in the digital world.

Common Forms of Cyber Vandalism

a) Website Defacement

Website defacement means changing the appearance or content of a website without the owner's permission.

Example: An attacker replaces an organization's homepage with offensive messages or unwanted images.

b) Data Destruction

It involves intentionally deleting, changing or damaging digital files, records or databases.

Example: An attacker deletes important files from a company's computer system.

c) Website or Service Disruption

Cyber vandals may deliberately disrupt websites, networks or online services, making them unavailable to users.

Example: An attack causes an organization's website to stop working for several hours.

d) Malware-Based Vandalism

Offenders may use malicious software to damage files, change system settings or disrupt computers.

Example: Destructive malware damages important files and prevents a computer from working properly.

e) Social Media or Online Account Vandalism

An offender may gain unauthorized access to an online account and change, damage or delete its content.

Example: A hacked social media account is used to post offensive messages or delete the owner's posts.

Conclusion

Cyber vandalism involves deliberately damaging or disrupting digital property. Its common forms include website defacement, data destruction, service disruption, malware-based damage and online account vandalism.
