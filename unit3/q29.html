Q29. Analyze the Vulnerabilities and Factors Contributing to Women's Victimization on Social Networks.

Introduction

Social networking platforms allow people to communicate, share information and connect with others.

However, women may experience online harassment, cyberstalking, impersonation, threats and misuse of personal information. Certain vulnerabilities and online factors can increase these risks.

1. Sharing Personal Information and Public Profiles

  • Public profiles may expose photographs, locations, contact details and daily activities.
  • Offenders may misuse this information for stalking, harassment or identity theft.

2. Fake Profiles and Anonymity

  • Offenders may use fake accounts to hide their real identities.
  • These accounts may be used to send threats, harass women or continue contacting them after being blocked.

3. Contact with Unknown People

  • Accepting friend requests or messages from strangers may increase exposure to online offenders.
  • Some offenders may gain trust through friendly conversations and later misuse personal information or commit fraud.

4. Misuse of Photos and Personal Content

  • Photographs and personal content may be copied, edited or shared without permission.
  • Such material may be used for impersonation, blackmail or damaging a woman's reputation.

5. Weak Privacy, Security and Awareness

  • Weak passwords, public privacy settings and lack of two-factor authentication can make accounts more vulnerable.
  • Limited awareness of fake accounts, privacy controls and reporting options may increase the risk of victimization.

6. Gender-Based Harassment

  • Women may receive abusive comments, unwanted sexual messages or threats because of their gender.
  • Such harassment may create fear, emotional stress and discourage participation on social networks.

Conclusion

Women's victimization on social networks can be influenced by public profiles, fake accounts, contact with strangers, misuse of photographs, weak security and gender-based harassment. These factors can increase exposure to online threats, harassment and exploitation.
