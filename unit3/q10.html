Q10. Describe Cyberbullying and Its Impact on Victims.

Introduction

Cyberbullying means using the internet or digital technology to repeatedly harass, insult, threaten, embarrass or harm another person.

It can take place through social media, messaging apps, emails, online games and other digital platforms.

Unlike traditional bullying, cyberbullying can happen at any time and from any place, and harmful content can quickly reach many people.

1. Common Forms of Cyberbullying

a) Abusive Messages

Sending repeated insulting, rude or threatening messages to a person.

Example: Repeatedly sending hurtful messages to a student through a messaging app.

b) Spreading Rumours

Posting or sharing false information about someone to damage their reputation.

c) Online Humiliation

Posting embarrassing comments, photos or videos to make fun of someone publicly.

d) Impersonation

Creating a fake account or using another person's account to post harmful content in their name.

e) Exclusion

Intentionally excluding someone from online groups, chats or activities to make them feel isolated.

f) Sharing Private Content

Sharing someone's private messages, photographs or videos without their permission to embarrass or harm them.

2. How Cyberbullying Occurs

Digital Platform
↓
Harmful Message / Post / Image
↓
Repeated Sharing or Harassment
↓
Victim Feels Threatened or Humiliated
↓
Emotional and Social Harm

3. Impact on Victims

a) Emotional Stress

Victims may experience sadness, fear, anger, embarrassment and stress.

b) Anxiety

A victim may constantly worry about receiving another harmful message or seeing another embarrassing post.

c) Loss of Confidence

Repeated insults and humiliation can reduce a person's self-confidence and self-esteem.

d) Social Isolation

Victims may avoid friends, social media or social activities because they feel embarrassed or unsafe.

e) Educational Problems

Students experiencing cyberbullying may have difficulty concentrating on studies, which can affect their academic performance.

f) Sleep Problems

Fear and stress caused by cyberbullying may disturb normal sleep.

g) Damage to Reputation

False rumours, embarrassing images or harmful posts can damage a person's reputation, especially when they are widely shared.

4. Cyberbullying and Its Effects

Form of Cyberbullying Possible Impact
Abusive messages Fear and stress
Spreading rumours Reputation damage
Public humiliation Embarrassment
Impersonation Loss of trust and privacy
Online exclusion Loneliness
Sharing private content Emotional distress and privacy loss

Conclusion

Cyberbullying is the use of digital platforms to harass, threaten, embarrass or harm another person. It can take many forms, including abusive messages, rumours, impersonation, exclusion and sharing private content. Its effects can be serious, causing fear, stress, anxiety, loss of confidence, social isolation and educational problems.
