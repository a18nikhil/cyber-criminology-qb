Q18. Discuss the Characteristics and Motivations of Internet Child Pornography Consumers.

Introduction

Internet Child Pornography Consumers are people who use the internet to view, download, store or share sexual abuse material involving children.

Such material is called Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) because it involves the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.

These consumers may show certain characteristics and motivations that help explain their behaviour.

1. Characteristics of Internet Child Pornography Consumers

There is no single fixed profile of such consumers. They may come from different age groups and social backgrounds.

Some common characteristics include:

  1. Regular Internet Use: They may spend considerable time online searching for or viewing illegal material.
  2. Secrecy and Fake Identities: Some use fake usernames or accounts to hide their identity and illegal activities.
  3. Repeated Viewing: Some consumers repeatedly view such material over time.
  4. Collection and Storage: Some download and keep illegal images or videos on digital devices.
  5. Interaction with Others: Some communicate with other offenders and may exchange illegal material through online groups.

2. Motivations of Internet Child Pornography Consumers

Different offenders may have different motivations.

  1. Sexual Interest: Some offenders may have a sexual interest in children, which motivates them to seek such material.
  2. Curiosity: For some individuals, the behaviour may begin with curiosity and later develop into repeated viewing.
  3. Sexual Gratification: Some offenders seek sexual pleasure or satisfaction from viewing such material.
  4. Habit and Repetition: Repeated viewing may become a strong habit, encouraging continued consumption.
  5. Feeling of Anonymity: Some offenders believe their online identity is hidden, which may reduce their fear of being caught.
  6. Group Influence: Contact with other offenders may encourage the behaviour and make it seem acceptable within those groups.
  7. Poor Self-Control: Some offenders may find it difficult to control their behaviour even when they understand that it is harmful and illegal.

Conclusion

Internet Child Pornography Consumers may show characteristics such as secrecy, repeated viewing, storing illegal material and interacting with other offenders. Their motivations may include sexual interest, curiosity, gratification, habit, perceived anonymity, group influence and poor self-control.
