Q18. Discuss the Characteristics and Motivations of Internet Child Pornography Consumers.

Introduction

Internet Child Pornography Consumers are people who use the internet to view, download, store, or share illegal sexual material involving children. Today, the term Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) is preferred because such material shows the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.

These consumers may show certain characteristics and motivations that help explain their online behaviour.

1. Characteristics of Internet Child Pornography Consumers

There is no single fixed profile of such consumers. They may come from different ages, occupations and social backgrounds. However, some common characteristics may include:

  1. Regular Internet Use: They may spend a large amount of time online searching for or viewing illegal material.
  2. Secrecy: They usually try to keep their activities hidden because they know the behaviour is illegal.
  3. Use of Fake Identities: Some may use fake usernames or accounts to hide their real identity.
  4. Repeated Viewing: Some consumers repeatedly view or collect such illegal material over time.
  5. Collection and Storage: Some may download and store illegal images or videos on digital devices.
  6. Interaction with Others: Some may communicate with other offenders who have similar illegal interests.
  7. Sharing of Material: Some consumers may exchange or distribute illegal material to others.

2. Motivations of Internet Child Pornography Consumers

Different offenders may have different motivations.

a) Sexual Interest

Some consumers may have a sexual interest in children, which motivates them to search for such material.

b) Curiosity

For some individuals, the behaviour may begin because of curiosity and later become repeated behaviour.

c) Sexual Gratification

Some offenders consume such material for sexual pleasure or satisfaction.

d) Habit and Repeated Behaviour

Repeated viewing may develop into a strong habit, causing the person to continue seeking such material.

e) Feeling of Anonymity

The internet may give offenders a feeling that their real identity is hidden. This can reduce their fear of being caught.

f) Influence of Online Groups

Communication with other offenders may encourage the behaviour. Such groups may wrongly make the offender feel that the behaviour is normal or acceptable.

g) Lack of Self-Control

Some offenders may find it difficult to control their urges and may continue the behaviour even when they know it is harmful and illegal.

3. Harmful Nature of This Behaviour

The consumption of such material is not a victimless activity. The material records the sexual abuse and exploitation of real children.

Viewing, possessing, producing or sharing such material can be a serious criminal offence. Continued demand for it can also contribute to further exploitation of children.

Conclusion

Internet Child Pornography Consumers do not have one fixed profile, but some may show characteristics such as secrecy, repeated viewing, collection of illegal material and interaction with other offenders. Their motivations can include sexual interest, curiosity, gratification, habit, perceived anonymity and online group influence. This behaviour is harmful because it is directly connected with the sexual abuse and exploitation of children.
