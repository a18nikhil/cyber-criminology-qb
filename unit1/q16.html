Q16. Describe the concept of Café Culture and Yahoo Boyism in Nigeria as a form of cybercrime subculture.

Introduction

Café Culture and Yahoo Boyism are related to the growth of internet fraud in Nigeria. When internet access became common through cyber cafés, some people started using these cafés for online fraud. Such people became popularly known as Yahoo Boys.

1. Café Culture

In the early days of the internet in Nigeria, many people did not have internet access at home. Therefore, they used cyber cafés to access the internet.

Most people used cyber cafés for normal purposes, but some used them for online fraud. They used cafés to:

  • Create fake accounts and identities.
  • Send fake emails and messages.
  • Contact people in other countries.
  • Carry out online scams.
  • Learn fraud methods from other people.

In this way, cyber cafés also became meeting and working places for some online fraudsters.

2. Yahoo Boyism

Yahoo Boyism refers to a type of online fraud mainly associated with some young internet fraudsters in Nigeria. These fraudsters are commonly called Yahoo Boys.

Their main aim is to trick people online and get money from them.

They may use fake names, fake photos, false stories and attractive offers to gain the trust of victims.

3. Common Activities of Yahoo Boys

  1. Advance-fee fraud: Asking for money first by making a false promise.
  2. Romance scam: Creating a fake love relationship to get money.
  3. Fake business offer: Giving false job, business or investment offers.
  4. Impersonation: Pretending to be another person.
  5. Phishing: Sending fake messages to steal personal information.

4. Yahoo Boyism as a Cybercrime Subculture

A subculture is a group of people who share similar ideas, habits and ways of doing things.

Yahoo Boyism is called a cybercrime subculture because some Yahoo Boys:

  • Work or communicate with other fraudsters.
  • Share ideas and fraud methods.
  • Learn new tricks from each other.
  • Use similar methods to cheat victims.
  • May see successful fraudsters as examples to follow.

The process can be shown as:

Internet / Cyber Café
↓
Meeting Other Fraudsters
↓
Learning Fraud Methods
↓
Online Scams
↓
Getting Money

5. Main Features of Yahoo Boyism

  1. Use of the internet: Online platforms are used to contact victims.
  2. Fake identity: Fraudsters may use fake names, photos and accounts.
  3. Tricking victims: They gain the victim's trust and then ask for money.
  4. Learning from others: Fraud methods may be shared among fraudsters.
  5. Money as the main aim: Most scams are carried out for financial gain.
  6. Victims from different countries: The internet allows them to target people anywhere in the world.

Conclusion

Café Culture helped some online fraudsters meet, communicate and carry out scams through the internet. Yahoo Boyism developed as a cybercrime subculture in which fraudsters use fake identities, false stories and online communication to cheat people for money.
