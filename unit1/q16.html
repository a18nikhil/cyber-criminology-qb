Q16. Describe the concept of Café Culture and Yahoo Boyism in Nigeria as a form of cybercrime subculture.

Introduction

Café Culture and Yahoo Boyism are associated with the growth of internet fraud in Nigeria. As internet access became available through cyber cafés, some young people began using these facilities for online scams. Such fraudsters became popularly known as Yahoo Boys.

1. Café Culture in Nigeria

In the early days of the internet in Nigeria, many people did not have internet access at home. Therefore, they visited cyber cafés to use the internet.

Most people used cyber cafés for normal activities, but some used them for online fraud.

These fraudsters used cyber cafés to:

  • Create fake online accounts and identities.
  • Contact victims from different countries.
  • Send fraudulent emails and messages.
  • Learn fraud methods from other fraudsters.
  • Carry out online scams.

Thus, some cyber cafés became places where fraudsters could meet, communicate and learn from one another.

2. Yahoo Boyism

Yahoo Boyism refers to a form of internet fraud associated with some young fraudsters in Nigeria, commonly called Yahoo Boys.

Their main aim is to deceive people online and obtain money from them.

They often use fake identities, false promises and emotional stories to gain the trust of victims.

Common fraud activities include:

  • Advance-fee fraud: Asking for money in advance through false promises.
  • Romance scams: Creating fake romantic relationships to obtain money.
  • Fake business offers: Using false jobs or investment opportunities to cheat people.
  • Impersonation: Pretending to be another person to commit fraud.
  • Phishing: Using fake messages or links to steal personal information.

3. Yahoo Boyism as a Cybercrime Subculture

A subculture is a group of people who share similar ideas, habits, beliefs and ways of behaving.

Yahoo Boyism is considered a cybercrime subculture because some fraudsters:

  • Form groups: They connect with other people involved in online fraud.
  • Share knowledge: They exchange ideas and methods of committing scams.
  • Learn from others: New members may learn fraud techniques from experienced fraudsters.
  • Influence one another: Friends and group members may encourage participation in fraud.
  • Value wealth and status: Successful fraudsters may be admired for their money and lifestyle.
  • Develop shared behaviour: Members may use similar fraud methods and view such activities as normal within their groups.

In this way, Yahoo Boyism is not just an individual criminal activity but also a group-based cybercrime culture.

Conclusion

Café Culture provided some fraudsters in Nigeria with internet access and opportunities to connect with others. Yahoo Boyism developed as a cybercrime subculture in which members share fraud methods, influence one another and use online scams for financial gain.
